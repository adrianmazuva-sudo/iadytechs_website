Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions.

How long does a typical repair take?

Most repairs are completed within 1-3 hours. Complex repairs may take 1-2 days. We'll always give you an estimated time before starting.

Do you offer warranty on repairs?

Yes! All our repairs come with a warranty period. Screen replacements and battery changes include a 30-day warranty.

Can you source specific products?

Absolutely! If we don't have what you're looking for in stock, we can source it for you. Just let us know your requirements.

Do you offer payment plans?

We accept cash, mobile money (EcoCash, OneMoney), and bank transfers. Contact us to discuss payment options for larger purchases.
